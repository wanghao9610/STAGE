%% manus/main.tex — STAGE manuscript entry point (one paper = one repo).
%%
%% Build: bash execs/run.sh          latexmk, out-of-tree; the PDF lands in
%%                                   wkdrs/builds/, never next to the sources.
%% Lint:  bash execs/scpts/lint.sh   deterministic checks; any todo marker in
%%                                   manus/ fails it — that is the point.
%%
%% Three files in stys/, the two loaded here first:
%%
%%   stys/stage.cls   the look — page geometry, the title panel, headings,
%%                    captions, floats, and the citation package. This is the
%%                    layer a venue's format replaces, in the copy
%%                    /stage-subm-packer convert generates from the kit under
%%                    cycls/<cycle>/template/ (conventions §10.4); the line
%%                    below stays \documentclass{stys/stage}. run.sh compiles
%%                    with manus/ as the working directory and puts manus/stys/
%%                    on TEXINPUTS.
%%
%%   stys/stage.sty   the authoring layer — \todo plus the macros the writing
%%                    skills emit into secs/ and tabs/. It survives every venue
%%                    swap; keep this line whatever the class becomes.
%%
%%   stys/stage.bst   the reference list — named by \bibliographystyle near the
%%                    end of this file rather than loaded here, and replaced by
%%                    a venue kit's own .bst the same way the class is.
%%
%% Class options: onecolumn | twocolumn, plus anon and anything article takes.
%% `anon` prints the title panel as "Anonymous Authors" and drops affiliations,
%% contribution notes, and the links row. That is the PDF half of double-blind;
%% the source half is ANON=true in .env, which makes lint.sh fail on identity in
%% typeset text anywhere under manus/, and on the \documentclass line below
%% until it carries anon. Comments are not scanned: a commented-out block stays
%% out of the PDF but ships with a source upload, so delete a real name from one
%% yourself. Keep the placeholders below anonymous until the camera-ready.
