%% manus/main.tex — STAGE manuscript entry point (one paper = one repo).
%%
%% Build: bash execs/run.sh          latexmk, out-of-tree; the PDF lands in
%%                                   wkdrs/builds/, never next to the sources.
%% Lint:  bash execs/scpts/lint.sh   deterministic checks; any todo marker in
%%                                   manus/ fails it — that is the point.
%%
%% Two layers, in load order:
%%
%%   stys/arxiv.cls   the look — page geometry, the title panel, headings,
%%                    captions, floats, bibliography style. This is the layer a
%%                    venue swap replaces: drop the venue's class and style
%%                    files into manus/stys/ and change the line below to, say,
%%                    \documentclass{stys/cvpr}. run.sh compiles with manus/ as
%%                    the working directory and puts manus/stys/ on TEXINPUTS.
%%
%%   stys/stage.sty   the authoring layer — \todo plus the macros the writing
%%                    skills emit into secs/ and tabs/. It survives every venue
%%                    swap; keep this line whatever the class becomes.
%%
%% Class options: onecolumn | twocolumn, plus anon and anything article takes.
%% `anon` prints the title panel as "Anonymous Authors" and drops affiliations,
%% contribution notes, and the links row. That is the PDF half of double-blind;
%% the source half is ANON=true in .env, which makes lint.sh fail on identity
%% anywhere under manus/ — comments included, because comments ship with a
%% source upload. Keep the placeholders below anonymous until the camera-ready.
